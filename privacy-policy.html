Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026. This page explains exactly what this site does and doesn't do with your data — in plain language, matching what the code actually does.

What's stored, and where

Every fund you add to the SIP Ledger, and every figure you enter on the SWP or SIP+SWP planners, is saved using your browser's local storage on your own device. None of it is sent to us or to any server we run — we don't have a database, a login system, or any way to see your data at all. Clearing your browser's site data, or using a different browser or device, will not show your saved entries — they never left your browser.

Third-party data this site calls

The live fund name search and NAV (Net Asset Value) check features send requests directly from your browser to api.mfapi.in, a public API for Indian mutual fund data (sourced from AMFI). Only the fund name you type, or a scheme code, is sent — no personal information. We don't control or operate that API; see mfapi.in for how it handles requests.

Cookies & advertising

This site may show advertisements served by third parties (such as Google AdSense). Those providers may use cookies or similar technology to show ads based on your visits to this and other sites. You can control ad personalization through your Google account settings, or opt out of personalized advertising generally at aboutads.info.

No financial advice

Every number this site shows is an illustrative calculation based on the assumptions you enter — expected return, inflation, withdrawal rate, and so on. It is not a guarantee, a recommendation, or financial advice. Actual mutual fund performance varies. Please make investment decisions with your own research or a qualified financial advisor.
